# Cortexity: brand and offer

## Offer
Cortexity designs and builds apps for iPhone, Android and web. The founder, Joseph, builds every project himself, one at a time.

## Core selling points
1. First version working in 21 days.
2. One fixed price, 100% satisfaction guaranteed.
3. Built by the founder himself, one project at a time.

Supporting points: weekly builds, the client owns the code, 30 days of free bug fixes after launch, shipped apps as proof (SLOWR, ReflexFlow, Pixelogram, IQ Calorie).

## Wording used on the site
- Guarantee: "100% satisfaction guarantee". Cortexity keeps working until the client is satisfied, at no extra charge.
- Pricing: price range on the call, formal fixed quote within 24 hours. No prices shown on the site.
- App names as written: "IQ Calorie", "SLOWR", "ReflexFlow", "Pixelogram".
- Platforms: iPhone, Android and web (never "iPhone only").

## Visual identity
- Colours: red `#E0201A`, ink `#1D1D1F`, black `#000000`, white. Greys `#F5F5F7`, `#6E6E73`.
- Type: SF Pro (system sans) for text; italic serif (New York) in red for the accent words in headlines.
- Satisfaction seal: red scalloped rosette, black centre, white "100%", "SATISFACTION / GUARANTEED" ring, "CORTEXITY" under the figure (`components/Seal.tsx`).
- Site imagery: real app screenshots only (`public/screens/`). Full design system: `DESIGN.md`.

## Qualified lead
Start date is not "Just exploring for now" and budget is not "Under $10,000". Everyone can book a call; Meta receives Schedule only for qualified bookings, Lead for every application.
